## What the owner recorded

- B1 retained a partial-return accounting bug after an alternative whole-file
  write was rejected and a later partial repair failed to include current-return
  quantities. Subsequent checks did not exercise the remaining zero-value case.
- B2 repeatedly alternated a cancellation/refund workaround between incompatible
  outcomes. The underlying pending-payment overwrite was identified near timeout
  but not repaired before exit.
- Workers noticed peers without establishing a PI repair agreement. All PI runs
  exhausted the steering notice allowance early, including successful B3; notice
  exhaustion alone therefore does not explain the failed runs.

These are the owner's recorded observations, not a new causal or transcript audit.
The proposed changes to context completeness, repair notices and notice budgets
remain **on hold**. Publishing this record does not resume that work or launch trials.
