Extend this shop's full-refund behavior to support partial refunds and durable retry safety, preserving the existing public contracts in README.md.

refund(order_id, request_id, quantity=None) returns amount and cumulative refunded in dollars, remaining units, and status (partially_refunded or refunded). A positive integer quantity returns that many not-yet-returned units; None returns the remainder. For original charged cents C, original quantity Q, and cumulative returned units n, cumulative refunded cents must be round-half-up(C*n/Q); this request refunds only the increase. Thus three odd items charged $2.02 refund $0.67, $0.68, $0.67 when returned one at a time. Never recompute refunds from today's prices/discounts. Restock exactly the returned units without altering other reservations.

Bind each request_id to its order and original quantity argument (including None). Identical retries return the original response, even after later refunds or save/load; conflicting reuse, nonpositive/noninteger quantities (including bool), and over-refunds raise ValueError without changing business state. Save/load must preserve refund progress, retry bindings/results and extra order fields. Legacy snapshots lacking a retry ledger must still load. Cached receipts must update after each refund, retain original purchase amounts, and remain detached from caller mutations. No new runtime dependencies.
